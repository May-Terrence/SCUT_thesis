\chapter{摘\texorpdfstring{\quad}{}要}

涵道风扇式无人机因空气动力效率高，安全性强，兼具垂直起降和水平巡航模态等优势，在许多场景下表现出了广泛的适用性。但是由于涵道风扇的罩状旋翼在飞行器周围流场中表现出强耦合效应以及不同飞行模态下的气动特性也不同等原因，基于模型的控制方法难以取得令人满意的性能。此外，关于涵道风扇式无人机的轨迹规划文献相对较少，这也不利于其进一步发展与应用。因此，本文针对涵道风扇式无人机的飞行控制与轨迹规划问题进行研究，聚焦于系统建模分析、姿态控制算法、位置控制算法和轨迹规划方法等四个方面，主要研究内容如下：

(1)基于牛顿-欧拉方法建立了涵道风扇式无人机的飞行控制刚体模型，并从涵道风扇、机身、涵道翼型、控制舵面和固定气动面等多个方面定量地分析了作用在机体上的力与力矩，最后建立了完备的非线性系统动态方程。

(2)提出结合增量非线性动态逆和优先级控制分配的涵道风扇式无人机姿态控制算法，该算法不依赖准确的系统模型，仅要求对输入通道建模。首先通过模型简化将力矩分为可控力矩和不可控力矩，可控力矩根据其产生机理直接计算，由模型误差和干扰产生的不可控力矩对姿态的影响通过陀螺仪高频采样在每个控制周期内补偿。然后采用优先级控制分配方法将系统力矩输入划分不同优先级，以求最大范围返回容许控制。

(3)提出基于增量非线性动态逆的涵道风扇式无人机位置控制算法，该算法结合提出的姿态控制形成外环-内环协同的扰动观测补偿机制。增量非线性动态逆位置控制算法依赖于加速度计的高频采样，通过加速度计测量值估计出机体受到的外力扰动，并在每个控制周期内对外力扰动进行补偿。然后基于三轴推力分量，使用一种几何解算方法计算出期望的机体姿态。

(4)提出一种基于RRT的改进路径规划算法C-RRT*，C-RRT*算法不仅通过建立两棵随机树彼此交替生长快速找出无碰撞的初始路径，而且还加入了贪婪策略、渐进优化机制和冗余节点删除策略，在提高搜索效率的同时也能提高搜索到的路径质量。针对涵道风扇式无人机的约束条件，使用B样条曲线对C-RRT*算法搜索到的初始路径进行平滑处理，并对速度和加速度进行动力学约束得到满足要求的轨迹。

最后，本文通过仿真实验或实际飞行实验对提出的飞行控制算法和轨迹规划算法进行验证，实验结果表明了提出的算法的有效性。

\chapter{Abstract}

Ducted fan UAV have demonstrated broad applicability across various scenarios due to their high aerodynamic efficiency, enhanced safety, and the ability to operate in both vertical takeoff and landing and horizontal cruise modes. However, the strong coupling effects induced by the ducted fan's shrouded rotor within the surrounding flow field, as well as the varying aerodynamic characteristics across different flight modes, pose significant challenges for model-based control approaches, often resulting in unsatisfactory performance. Furthermore, the literature on trajectory planning for ducted fan UAV remains relatively scarce, hindering their further development and practical applications. In response to these challenges, this paper investigates the flight control and trajectory planning of ducted fan UAV, focusing on four key aspects: system modeling and analysis, attitude control algorithms, position control algorithms, and trajectory planning methods. The main research contributions are as follows:

(1) A rigid-body flight control model for the ducted fan UAV is established based on the Newton-Euler method. The forces and moments acting on the UAV are quantitatively analyzed from multiple perspectives, including the ducted fan, fuselage, duct airfoil, control surfaces, and fixed aerodynamic surfaces. Finally, a complete set of nonlinear dynamic equations is formulated to comprehensively describe the system dynamics.

(2) An attitude control algorithm for ducted fan UAV is proposed, integrating Incremental Nonlinear Dynamic Inversion with priority-based control allocation. This approach does not rely on an accurate system model and only requires modeling of the input channels. First, by simplifying the model, the moments are categorized into controllable and uncontrollable components. The controllable moments are directly computed based on their generation mechanisms, while the effects of uncontrollable moments—arising from model uncertainties and external disturbances—are compensated within each control cycle using high-frequency gyroscope sampling. Subsequently, a priority-based control allocation method is employed to assign different priority levels to the system's moment inputs, aiming to maximize the feasible control authority.

(3) A position control algorithm for ducted fan UAV is proposed based on Incremental Nonlinear Dynamic Inversion. This algorithm, in conjunction with the proposed attitude control method, establishes an outer-loop and inner-loop cooperative disturbance observation and compensation mechanism. The INDI-based position control algorithm relies on high-frequency sampling of accelerometers to estimate external force disturbances acting on the UAV. These disturbances are then compensated for within each control cycle. Subsequently, based on the three-axis thrust components, a geometric solving method is employed to compute the desired body attitude.

(4) An improved path planning algorithm based on RRT, named C-RRT*, is proposed. The C-RRT* algorithm not only rapidly finds a collision-free initial path by alternately growing two random trees but also incorporates a greedy strategy, progressive optimization mechanism, and redundant node removal strategy to enhance search efficiency while improving path quality. Considering the constraints of the ducted fan UAV, the initial path generated by the C-RRT* algorithm is smoothed using B-spline curves. Additionally, velocity and acceleration constraints are applied to ensure the generated trajectory satisfies the dynamic requirements.

Finally, the proposed flight control algorithms and trajectory planning algorithm are validated through simulation experiments or real-flight tests. The experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed algorithms.
